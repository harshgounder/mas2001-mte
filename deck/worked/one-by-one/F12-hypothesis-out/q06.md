PART 1: THE QUESTION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

```
t-test is used when we have:
   a. Large sample
   b. Small sample
   c. Infinite sample
   d. No sample
```

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
PART 2: THE FULL ANSWER, FROM ZERO (every step, nothing assumed)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

ANSWER: (b) small sample. (t is the small-sample test with unknown sigma; z is for large
samples or known sigma.)
