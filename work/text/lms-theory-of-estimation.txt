• Statistical inference is the process by which we acquire information about populations

   – Estimation.

Concepts of Estimation
• The objective of estimation is to determine the value of a population parameter on the basis
  of a sample statistic.
• There are two types of estimators
   – Point Estimator
   – Interval estimator

Answer the following:

               Properties of a Good Point Estimator
(ii) Finding value of λ for T₃ to be unbiased
Let T₃ = λ (X₁ + X₂ + X₃) / 3
For unbiasedness, E(T₃) = μ
E(T₃) = (λ / 3)[E(X₁) + E(X₂) + E(X₃)]
= (λ / 3)(μ + μ + μ) = λμ
For unbiasedness: λμ = μ ⇒ λ = 1

(iii) Consistency of T₃
With λ = 1, T₃ = (X₁ + X₂ + X₃) / 3 = X̄ (sample mean).
Since the sample mean is a consistent estimator of the population
mean, T₃ is a consistent estimator.

(iv) Variance of the estimators:
