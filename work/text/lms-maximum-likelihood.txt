Suppose we have a random sample 𝑋1 , 𝑋2 , … , 𝑋𝑛 whose assumed

where 𝑥1 , 𝑥2 , … , 𝑥𝑛 are the observed values of the random sample.

i.e., Our goal is to find a good estimate of 𝜃 using the data
                               𝑥1 , 𝑥2 , … , 𝑥𝑛
that we obtained from our specific random sample.
              Maximum Likelihood Estimation (MLE)
• Maximum Likelihood Estimation (MLE) is a technique used
  for estimating the parameters of a given distribution using
  some observed data.

• For example, if a population is known to follow a normal
  distribution, but the mean and variance are unknown, MLE
  can be used to estimate them using a limited sample of the
  population.

• This is done by finding particular values of the mean and
  variance so that the observed data is the most likely result to
  have occurred.

• This is the reason why it is called the likelihood function.
