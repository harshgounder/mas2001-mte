## Part (iii): No Defective Pen
Find P(X = 0) - probability that all 12 pens are non-defective
Apply PMF with x = 0
$$P(X = 0) = \binom{12}{0} \times (0.1)^0 \times (0.9)^{12}$$
Evaluate Each Term
$\binom{12}{0} = 1$
$(0.1)^0 = 1$
$(0.9)^{12} = 0.2824$
Multiply the terms
$$1 \times 1 \times 0.2824 = 0.2824$$
✓ P(X = 0) = 0.2824
