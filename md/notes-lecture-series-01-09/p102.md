## Expectation & Distribution parameters (cont...)

**Properties of Variance**
Theorem: $Var[X] = E[X^2] - (E[X])^2$
Proof: By def,
$Var[X] = E[(X - \mu)^2] = E[X^2 - 2\mu X + \mu^2]$
Now using the rules of expectation,
we obtain
$Var(X) = E[X^2] - 2\mu E[X] + \mu^2$
Since the symbols $\mu$ and $E[X]$ are interchangeable.
