## Continuous Random Variables

**Example:** Suppose that the r.v $X$ is continuous, Let the pdf $f$ is given by:

$$f(x) = \begin{cases} 2x & 0 < x < 1 \\ 0, & \text{elsewhere} \end{cases}$$

**Clearly**, $f(x) \geq 0$ and also

$$\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} f(x)dx = \int_{0}^{1} 2x dx = 1$$
