## Discrete Random Variable

$$ \mathrm{E[h_3(X)]} = \sum_{x=1}^{6} h_3(x) \cdot p(x) = (-1)\left(\frac{1}{15}\right) + \dots + (3)\left(\frac{2}{15}\right) = 2.4667 $$

Similarly, $$ \mathrm{E[h_4(X)]} = \sum_{x=1}^{6} h_4(x) \cdot p(x) = (-2)\left(\frac{1}{15}\right) + \dots + (4)\left(\frac{2}{15}\right) = 2.6667 $$

Ordering 4 copies gives slightly higher revenue, on the average.
