## Discrete Random Variable

Example A contractor is required by a county planning department to submit one, two, three, four, or five forms (depending on the nature of the project) in applying for a building permit. Let $Y =$ the number of forms required of the next applicant. The probability that $y$ forms are required is known to be proportional to $y$: that is, $p(y) = ky$ for $y = 1, \dots, 5$. 

a. What is the value of $k$? [*Hint:* $\sum_{y=1}^5 p(y) = 1$.]  
b. What is the probability that at most three forms are required?  
c. What is the probability that between two and four forms (inclusive) are required?  
d. Could $p(y) = y^2/50$ for $y = 1, \dots, 5$ be the pmf of $Y$?
