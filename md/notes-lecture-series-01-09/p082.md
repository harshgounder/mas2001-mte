## Discrete Random Variable

Ex. Two fair six-sided dice are tossed independently. Let $M =$ the maximum of the two tosses (so $M(1, 5) = 5, M(3, 3) = 3$, etc.).

a. What is the pmf of $M$? [Hint: First determine $p(1)$, then $p(2)$, and so on.]

b. Determine the cdf of $M$ and graph it.
