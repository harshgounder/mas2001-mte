## Discrete Random Variable

Let $h(X)$ denote the net revenue (sales revenue - order cost) as a function of $X$. Then $h_3(X)$ and $h_4(X)$ are the net revenue for 3 and 4 copies purchased, respectively. For $x = 1$ or 2 , $h_3(X) = 2x - 3$, but at $x = 3,4,5,6$ the revenue plateaus. Following similar reasoning, $h_4(X) = 2x - 4$ for $x=1,2,3$, but plateaus at 4 for $x = 4,5,6$.

| x | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| $h_3(x)$ | -1 | 1 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 3 |
| $h_4(x)$ | -2 | 0 | 2 | 4 | 4 | 4 |
| $p(x)$ | $\frac{1}{15}$ | $\frac{2}{15}$ | $\frac{3}{15}$ | $\frac{4}{15}$ | $\frac{3}{15}$ | $\frac{2}{15}$ |
