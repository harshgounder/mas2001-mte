## Some Relations from Set Theory

Sometimes $A$ and $B$ have no outcomes in common, so that the intersection of $A$ and $B$ contains no outcomes.

**Definition**

Let $\phi$ denote the *null event* (the event consisting of no outcomes whatsoever).
When $A \cap B = \phi$, $A$ and $B$ are said to be **mutually exclusive** or **disjoint** events.
