## Random variable

Example: Suppose that we toss two coins and consider the sample space associated with the experiment $S = HH, HT, TH, TT$, define the random variable $X$ as follows:
$X$: Number of heads obtained in the two tosses
Hence, $X(HH) = 2, X(TT) = 0, X(TH) = 1, X(HT) = 1$
