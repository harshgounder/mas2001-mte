## Some Relations from Set Theory

2. The **union** of two events $A$ and $B$, denoted by $A \cup B$ and read “$A$ or $B$,” is the event consisting of all outcomes that are *either in $A$ or in $B$ or in both events* (so that the union includes outcomes for which both $A$ and $B$ occur as well as outcomes for which exactly one occurs) that is, all outcomes in at least one of the events.
