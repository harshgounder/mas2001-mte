## Discrete Random Variable

a. $\quad p(1) = P(M = 1) = P[(1,1)] = \frac{1}{36}$
$\quad \quad p(2) = P(M = 2) = P[(1,2) \text{ or } (2,1) \text{ or } (2,2)] = \frac{3}{36}$
$\quad \quad p(3) = P(M = 3) = P[(1,3) \text{ or } (2,3) \text{ or } (3,1) \text{ or } (3,2) \text{ or } (3,3)] = \frac{5}{36}$
Similarly, $p(4) = \frac{7}{36}$, $p(5) = \frac{9}{36}$, and $p(6) = \frac{11}{36}$

b. $\quad F(m) = 0 \text{ for } m < 1, \frac{1}{36} \text{ for } 1 \le m < 2,$

$\quad \quad F(m) = \begin{cases} 0 & m < 1 \\ \frac{1}{36} & 1 \le m < 2 \\ \frac{4}{36} & 2 \le m < 3 \\ \frac{9}{36} & 3 \le m < 4 \\ \frac{16}{36} & 4 \le m < 5 \\ \frac{25}{36} & 5 \le m < 6 \\ 1 & m \ge 6 \end{cases}$
