## Discrete Random Variable

Example
A random variable $X$ has the following probability function:
| Values of X, x: | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| P(x) | 0 | k | 2k | 2k | 3k | K² | 2k² | 7k²+k |
(i) Find $k$, (ii) Evaluate $P(X < 6)$, $P(X \geq 6)$, and $P(0 < X < 5)$ (iii) determine the distribution function of $X$ (iv) if $P(X \leq a) > \frac{1}{2}$, find the minimum value of $a$.
