## Probability Distributions for Continuous Variables

* That is, the probability that $X$ takes on a value in the interval $[a, b]$ is the area above this interval and under the graph of the density function, as illustrated in figure.

Figure: A plot showing a density curve on a coordinate system with horizontal axis $x$ and vertical axis $f(x)$. A bell-shaped curve is drawn, and the area under the curve between $x = a$ and $x = b$ is shaded in gray. The labels $a$ and $b$ are marked on the $x$-axis. The caption below the figure reads: $P(a \leq X \leq b) =$ the area under the density curve between $a$ and $b$.

* The graph of $f(x)$ is referred to as the *density curve*.
