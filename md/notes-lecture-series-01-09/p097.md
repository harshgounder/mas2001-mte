## Expectation & Distribution parameters (cont...)

**Definition:** If a discrete random variable $X$ has mean $\mu$, its variance $Var(X)$ or $\sigma^2$ is defined by

$$Var(X) = E[(X - \mu)^2]$$

The standard deviation $\sigma$ is the non-negative square root of $Var(X)$.
