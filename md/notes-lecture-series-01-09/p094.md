## Discrete Random Variable

**EX.** Let $X$ be a random variable with the following probability distribution :

| x | -3 | 6 | 9 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| $P(X = x)$ | 1/6 | 1/2 | 1/3 |

Find $E(X)$ and $E(X^2)$ and using the laws of expectation, evaluate $E(2X + 1)^2$.

**Ans.** $E(X)=11/2$, $E(X^2) = 93/2$, 209
