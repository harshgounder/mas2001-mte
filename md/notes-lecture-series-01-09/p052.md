## Random variable

**Example**
* E = Tossing the fair coin
    With $S = \{H, T\}$, define an rv $X$ by

    $X(H) = 1 \qquad X(T) = 0$

    The rv $X$ takes whether (1) or not (0)

* **Definition**
* Any random variable whose only possible values are 0 and 1 is called a **Bernoulli random variable**.
