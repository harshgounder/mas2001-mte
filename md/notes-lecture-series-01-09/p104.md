## Discrete Random Variable
**Proposition**
$$V(aX + b)=\sigma^2_{aX+b} = a^2\sigma^2 \quad \text{and} \quad \sigma_{aX+b} = |a|\sigma_x$$
In Particular
$$\sigma_{aX} = |a|\sigma_x, \quad \sigma_{X+b} =\sigma_X$$
The absolute value is necessary because $a$ might be negative, yet a standard deviation cannot be.
Usually multiplication by $a$ corresponds to a change in the unit of measurement (e.g. dollars to euros).
