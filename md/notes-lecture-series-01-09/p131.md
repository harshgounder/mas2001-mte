## Continuous Random Variables

The **cumulative distribution function (cdf)** $F(x)$ for a discrete rv $X$ gives, for any specified number $x$, the probability $P(X \le x)$ .

It is obtained by summing the pmf $p(y)$ over all possible values $y$ satisfying $y \le x$.

The cdf of a continuous rv gives the same probabilities $P(X \le x)$ and is obtained by integrating the pdf $f(y)$ between the limits
$x$.
