## Discrete Random Variable

*   **Probability Mass Function (p.m.f):** The **probability distribution** or **probability mass function** (pmf) of a discrete rv is defined for every number $x$ by $p(x) = P(X = x) = P(\text{all } s \in S: X(s) = x)$.
*   The density function of a random variable completely describe the behavior of the variable.
*   In words, for every possible value $x$ of the random variable, the pmf specifies the probability of observing that value when the experiment is performed.
*   The necessary and sufficient conditions
    (i) $p(x) \ge 0$ and
    (ii) $\sum_{\text{all possible } x} p(x) = 1$ are required of any pmf.
