## Continuous Random Variables

The variance and standard deviation give quantitative measures of how much spread there is in the distribution or population of $x$ values.

Again $\sigma$ is roughly the size of a typical deviation from . Computation of $\sigma^2$ is facilitated by using the same shortcut formula employed in the discrete case.

**Proposition**

$$V(X) = E(X^2) - [E(X)]^2$$
