## Limitations of Statistics

*   Statistics is not suited to the study of qualitative phenomenon
*   Statistics does not study individuals
*   Statistical laws are not exact (Universal true)
*   Statistics is liable to be misused

*Example:*
A survey on "customer satisfaction" is conducted only among loyal customers and ignores dissatisfied ones, leading to falsely positive results.
