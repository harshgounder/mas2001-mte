SECTION B

Q1. A small IT support desk has 3 technicians available for field visits on a day-to-day basis. The number of demands for a technician is known to follow a Poisson distribution with a mean of 2.5. Find the probability of a day on which (i) there is no demand for a technician and (ii) the demand is rejected.
Ans: 0.0821, 0.2424

Q2. A random variable $X$ is uniformly distributed between 10 and 20. Find the exact probability that $X$ is greater than 16.
Ans: 0.4

Q3. Given a normal distribution with a mean $\mu = 50$ and standard deviation $\sigma = 10$, find the probability that the variable $X$ assumes a value between 45 and 62. (Given standard normal area $\phi(0.5) = 0.1915$ and $\phi(1.2) = 0.3849$)
Ans: 0.5764

Q4. In a quality control test on 5000 lithium-ion batteries, it was found that the battery life was normally distributed with an average life of 1500 hours and an SD of 100 hours. Estimate the number of batteries likely to last for (i) More than 1660 hours, (ii) Less than 1380 hours, (iii) More than 1350 hours but less than 1680 hours. Given $\phi(1.2) = 0.3849$, $\phi(1.5) = 0.4332$, $\phi(1.6) = 0.4452$, $\phi(1.8) = 0.4641$. (Ans.: (i) 274, (ii) 576, (iii) 4487)

Q5. If $X$ has an exponential distribution with mean 2, find $P(X < 1 | X < 2)$. (Ans: 0.5679.)

Q6. In a distribution exactly normal, 10% of the items are under 30 and 85% are under 65. What are the mean and standard deviation of the distribution? Given $\phi(1.04) = 0.35$ and $\phi(1.28) = 0.4$. Ans: 49.31 and 15.09

Q7. The time (in hours) required to repair a watch is exponentially distributed with parameter $\lambda = \frac{1}{2}$. (i) What is the Probability that the repair times exceeds 2 hours? (ii) What is the probability that a repair takes more than 11 hours given that its duration exceeds 8 hours? (Ans: (i) $e^{-1}$ (ii) $e^{-1.5}$)

Q8. A random variable X is uniformly distributed over $(-3, 3)$, Compute (i) $P(X < 2)$, (ii) $P(|X| < 2)$, (iii) $P(|X - 2| < 2)$, and (iv) Find $K$ for which $P(X > K) = 1/3$. (Ans: 5/6, 2/3, 1/2, and K = 1)
