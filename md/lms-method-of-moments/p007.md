## EXAMPLE 1 — POISSON DISTRIBUTION

**Step-by-Step Derivation**
Put $r = 1$

$\mu'_1 = \lambda$ (First population moment about origin)

$m'_1 = \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i = \bar{X}$

$\mu'_1 = m'_1$

**Solve for $\lambda$**

$$\lambda = \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i$$

$$\lambda = \bar{X}$$

$$\hat{\lambda}_{MoM} = \bar{X}$$

$$\hat{\lambda}_{MoM} = \bar{X}$$
