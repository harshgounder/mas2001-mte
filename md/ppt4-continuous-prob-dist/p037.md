## Finding the X value

2. Convert to X units using the formula:

$$
\begin{aligned}
X &= \mu + Z\sigma \\
&= 8.0 + (-0.84)5.0 \\
&= 3.80
\end{aligned}
$$

So 20% of the values from a distribution with mean 8.0 and standard deviation 5.0 are less than 3.80
