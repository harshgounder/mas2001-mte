## Cumulative Normal Distribution

* For a normal random variable $X$ with mean $\mu$ and variance $\sigma^2$, i.e., $X \sim N(\mu, \sigma^2)$, the cumulative distribution function is

$$F(x_0) = P(X \le x_0)$$
