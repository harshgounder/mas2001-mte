0, & elsewhere
\end{cases}
$$

(ii) What is probability that none of three of the original tubes will have to be replaced during that first 150 hours of operation?

(iv) What is the maximum number of tubes that may be inserted into a set so that there is a probability of 0.5 that after 150 hours of service all of them are still functioning?
