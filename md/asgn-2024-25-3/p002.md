## SECTION-C

C1 Let $x_1 = 0.7, x_2 = 1.5, x_3 = 0.3, x_4 = 0.5, x_5 = 3.4$  
be a sample of size 5 from a distribution having pdf

$$
f(x,\theta)=\theta e^{-\theta x},\ x>0,\ \theta>0.
$$

Find the MLE of $\theta$.

C2 Let $x_1,x_2,\ldots\ldots\ldots\ldots\ldots,x_n$ be a random sample of n observations from a population having pdf

$$
f(x,\theta)=\theta e^{-\theta x},\ x>0.
$$

The prior distribution of $\theta$ is

$$
f_1(\theta)=\frac{1}{\theta},\ 0<\theta<\infty.
$$

Find the Bayes estimator of $\theta$.

C3 A random sample of size 10 was drawn from a normal population with an unknown mean and a variance of $44.1$ (inch)$^2$. If the observations are (in inches): 65, 71, 80, 76, 78, 82, 68, 72, 65 and 81, obtain the 95% confidence interval for the population mean.  
(tabulated value=2.262).

C4 For a $N(\mu,\sigma^2)$ population with variance equal to 9, Construct a 95% confidence interval for the parametric function $r(\theta)=2\mu+7$, based on the random sample size 25, giving a sample mean of 30.  
(tabulated value=1.96).

C5 Let X be a binomial random variable with parameters n and p.  
where

$$
f_1(p)=1,\ 0<p<1.
$$

Find the Bayes estimator of p.
