## Source defects and cautions

1. **2025 ETE QA3 calls worker salary binomial.** Salary is not a binomial count. The intended
   calculation is plainly for $B(180,1/3)$, whose mean and variance are 60 and 40, but the
   application noun is invalid.
2. **2024 ETE C2(a) has an ambiguous alternative.** It first asks whether there is “any
   difference,” then says the additional protein “has increased” weight. Those imply two-sided
   and one-sided tests respectively. A solution must state which alternative it uses.
3. **2025 ETE QB3 prints $E(2X+1)^2$.** This can mean $[E(2X+1)]^2$ or
   $E[(2X+1)^2]$. The phrase “using the laws of expectation” and the request for $E(X^2)$
   suggest the second, but the brackets should have been printed.
4. **Re-sessional QB2 crosses the page break.** Page 1 ends at “the demand is”; page 2 begins
   “rejected.” The assembled document preserves the complete sentence in reading order.
