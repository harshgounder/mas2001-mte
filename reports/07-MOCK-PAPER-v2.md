# Mock MTE paper v2, real A/B/C format

MAS2001 Statistics and Probability. 30 marks. 90 minutes. Closed book.

All questions are compulsory. Missing data, if any, may be assumed suitably. Calculator is
allowed. This structure matches both supplied MTE papers: Section A has 3 questions of 2
marks, Section B has 4 questions of 4 marks, and Section C has one 8-mark question.

Do not read `08-MOCK-SOLUTIONS-v2.md` before sitting the paper.

## Section A, 6 marks

### Q1, 2 marks

For a random variable X, the cumulative distribution function is

1. $P(X=x)$
2. $P(X\leq x)$
3. $P(X\geq x)$
4. $P(X>x)$

### Q2, 2 marks

Chebyshev's inequality applies to

1. only normal distributions
2. any distribution with finite mean and finite variance
3. only discrete distributions
4. only continuous distributions

### Q3, 2 marks

The standard error of a sample mean is halved when the sample size changes from

1. 25 to 50
2. 25 to 75
3. 25 to 100
4. 25 to 125

## Section B, 16 marks

### Q4, Chebyshev, 4 marks

Test marks have mean 70 and variance 25.

1. Find the minimum proportion of students scoring between 60 and 80. (2)
2. Find an upper bound for the probability that a student scores below 55 or above 85. (2)

### Q5, binomial, 4 marks

Eight percent of components produced by a machine are defective. A random sample of 15
components is drawn. Let X be the number of defective components.

1. State the distribution of X with its parameters. (1)
2. Find $P(X=1)$. (1)
3. Find $P(X\geq1)$. (1)
4. Find the mean and variance of X. (1)

### Q6, normal, 4 marks

Component lifetime X is normally distributed with mean 50 and variance 100.

1. Find $P(X<65)$. (1)
2. Find $P(X>35)$. (1)
3. Find x such that $P(X<x)=0.90$. (2)

Use $\Phi(1.50)=0.9332$ and $\Phi^{-1}(0.90)=1.2816$.

### Q7, estimation, 4 marks

Let $X_1,X_2,X_3$ be an independent random sample from a population with mean $\mu$ and
variance $\sigma^2$. Consider

$$T_1=X_1,\qquad T_2=\frac{X_1+X_2}{2},\qquad
T_3=\frac{X_1+X_2+X_3}{3},\qquad T_4=\frac{X_1+X_2+X_3}{2}.$$

1. Identify the unbiased estimators and give the bias of $T_4$. (2)
2. Among the unbiased estimators, identify the most efficient one. (1)
3. Explain why the sample-mean sequence is consistent while $T_1$ is not. (1)

## Section C, 8 marks

### Q8, continuous and discrete models, 4+4 marks

1. Let X be uniform on $(2,8)$. Derive its CDF for all real x and find $P(X<5)$. (4)
2. Let Y be Poisson with mean 3. Find $P(Y=0)$, $P(Y\geq1)$, $E(Y)$ and $Var(Y)$. (4)
