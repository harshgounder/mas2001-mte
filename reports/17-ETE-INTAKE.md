# ETE and re-sessional paper intake (odd, even, summer, re-sess)

Seven end-term and re-sessional papers were enumerated on 16 Sep 2026, because they are OUR
course papers and several blocks fall inside MTE syllabus (lectures 1 to 21). Counting rule
matches the count register: one block per numbered question, multi-part counts once.

Scope verdicts:
- IN: fits MTE syllabus content (chapters 1 to 4 concepts, distributions, Chebyshev, CLT,
  estimator properties, sufficiency).
- BOUNDARY: confidence interval construction or concept; kept with flag, per the count
  register boundary ruling ("Point and Interval estimation" is on the MTE syllabus list).
- OUT: hypothesis testing, t-test, chi-square, ANOVA, MLE, method of moments, Bayesian,
  type I and II error theory.
- PARTIAL: one block whose parts split across IN and OUT; the usable part is named.
